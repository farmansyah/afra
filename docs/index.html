Everything between your idea and the journal

The tools researchers juggle in separate apps, in one place, and built to save AI tokens: most features use none at all.

⌕

Find the right sources

  • Discover: paste your title and get up to 200 ranked papers, including synonym and citation-trail matches
  • Deep Research: an agent that writes a cited literature report
  • Quality filters: Q1–Q4, impact, open access, retracted papers hidden
  • Snowballing: references, cited-by and related papers
✓

Back every argument

  • Evidence finder: type your claim and see which papers support, partially support or contradict it
  • Verbatim quote from each abstract, checked word for word
  • One click inserts the citation right after your sentence
◇

Find your gap & novelty

  • Gap Finder: breaks your topic into facets and counts what has been published for each combination
  • Table of the closest prior work and what each one did not study
  • Drafts gaps, a novelty statement, research questions and an Introduction paragraph
✎

Write with a guide

  • Paper Writer: style guide, evidence-backed gaps, outline, then a 35-point simulated reviewer check before drafting
  • Section-by-section drafting with quality checks
  • Manuscript upload: see what's missing and which claims still need sources
▤

A real reference manager

  • PDFs with a built-in reader, plus quotes and notes with page numbers
  • Folders, smart collections and duplicate merging
  • Zotero sync, Mendeley/EndNote via BibTeX & RIS
⬇

Submit with confidence

  • Word export: APA 7, IEEE two-column, journal article, Skripsi/Tesis (BAB I, Daftar Pustaka)
  • Reference checker: catches invented DOIs, retracted papers and wrong metadata
  • Journal fit: word limits, required sections, scope fit

Install in two minutes

No programming needed. A.F.R.A opens in your web browser and runs on your computer.

Windows

  1. Download and unzip it into a folder you'll keep.
  2. Double-click start.bat. If Python is missing, the download page opens: install it and tick “Add python.exe to PATH”, then double-click start.bat again.
  3. A.F.R.A opens in your browser, and a desktop shortcut is created for next time.

Mac

  1. Download and unzip it, e.g. into Documents.
  2. Open AFRA.app (the blue AF icon). First time only: right-click → Open, or System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
  3. If Python is missing, a message explains what to do. Then drag AFRA.app to your Dock: one click from now on, no windows to keep open.

Then open Settings, pick your AI provider and paste your key. The Balanced preset picks a strong writing model and a cheap fast model for you.

Questions

Is A.F.R.A free?

Yes, the app is free to download and use. AI features use your own API key, so you pay your provider directly (often cents per paper), or use a free local model through Ollama. Literature search, quality filters, snowballing, the reference checker and many more features use no AI at all.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your computer, in A.F.R.A's data folder: your library, PDFs, drafts and API keys. It makes automatic backups every 6 hours and before anything is deleted. Only your search queries go to the scholarly databases, and only your prompts go to the AI provider you choose.

Does it write my paper for me?

It assists. It finds and organises evidence, checks quality and drafts text you then revise. You stay the author, and A.F.R.A never invents data or results. It includes a similarity check and an AI-use disclosure generator, because most journals accept AI assistance when it's disclosed.

Can I use it online, without installing?

Not at the moment. A.F.R.A runs on your own computer so that your unpublished work stays private. This website is its homepage.

Which journals and formats are supported?

Word export with APA 7, IEEE (two-column), a general journal-article layout, a research-report layout and the Indonesian Skripsi/Tesis format. References in APA, IEEE, Harvard, Chicago, Vancouver or MLA. The Journal fit checker reads any journal's author guidelines.
